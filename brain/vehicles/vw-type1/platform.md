# Type 1 platform

No ladder frame: two floor pans welded to a central tunnel, the "chapéu de napoleão" fork anchoring
the front axle beam, mid and rear crossmembers, a rounded tunnel spine. 10 members, metres in the
vehicle frame, anchored on model-1980 measurements (axles z ≈ ±1.24, track ±0.66, wheel centre y ≈ 0.35).
Enabled only on model-1980 today; the user wants it on the 1971–85 body (1973) → [T-0003](../../roadmap/T-0003-platform-from-hubs.md).

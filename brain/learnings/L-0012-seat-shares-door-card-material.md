# The seat shares the door card's material

In the 1973 cabin mesh the seat is mostly `Details_MAT_127` — the same material as the door card — and
its bolster overlaps the door zone. Excluding by material alone left seat pieces on the door. A density
scan of that material along X revealed the physical gap (x 0.45–0.48) to cut in.
